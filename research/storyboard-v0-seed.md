# Solaro — "From the first spark" — storyboard seed (v0, art director's first pass)

Format: 1080x1920, 60 fps, ~14.8 s (888 frames). Pure white (#FFFFFF) background. Ink #1C1C1C. Brand orange gradient #FF5E2B -> #FF3D00.
Music grid idea: 120 BPM -> 1 beat = 30 frames, 1 bar = 120 frames = 2 s. 7.4 bars.

## Core idea — one orange element evolves through the whole film
A single orange element is the protagonist and never leaves the screen. It is built from the Solaro shape language
(every shape in the mark is a 204-unit "cell" = rounded square with per-corner radii 0 / ~24 / 102 and an optional hole),
so every transformation is an exact parametric morph:

  spark dot -> ember -> FLAME (teardrop cell, square corner pointing up) -> lightning bolt (orange stroke) ->
  i-dot of "lighting" -> LIGHT BULB (same teardrop rotated 180 deg, corner down + tiny base cells) ->
  SUN (bulb glass rounds into the RING cell, hole opens) -> SOLAR PANEL (ring squares off into a tile, splits into a cell grid) ->
  the 4-cell Solaro MARK -> logo lockup.

Around it, black & white archival imagery flashes (engravings, museum objects, photos), each image positioned so its
"hot spot" (the spark point, the flame, the bulb, the sun) sits exactly under the orange element -> match-cut montage.

## Beats (frames @60fps)
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-36 | open | Solaro mark on screen; the 4 cells round off into circles and collapse into one orange dot (spark) at centre. |
| 36-70 | spark | two flint stones (cutouts) slam together on the dot; burst of orange spark strokes + flying dots; word **spark** pops. |
| 70-130 | spark montage | 6-8 frame cuts: hand axe, flint & steel, bow-drill engraving, cave hand stencil, sculpted hand - dot pulses/grows on each hot spot. |
| 130-250 | fire | dot morphs into a flickering FLAME cell; word **fire** with the flame as its i-dot; montage of lamps/torches/Prometheus/candle with the flame cell placed on each flame; 2x2 grid of cell-masked images at once. |
| 250-370 | lightning | flame snaps to a dot, orange bolt paints down from the top (flash); lightning photo with the bolt traced orange; Franklin's kite, Leyden jars grid, electrostatic machine spark, Volta pile. **lightning** -> **lighting** (the extra n is zapped out, letters close the gap). |
| 370-490 | electric age | the orange i-dot of "lighting" grows into a BULB over Edison's patent drawing; row of historic bulbs lighting up one by one; Tesla coil, insulators, pylons with orange current running along the wires, city lights. |
| 490-610 | sun | bulb glass rounds into the RING = the sun; zoom through its hole; inside the hole sun imagery changes every ~6 frames (Kircher 1665, Scheiner sunspots, Trouvelot, sunflower spiral, eclipse, NASA SDO) while an orbit line + dot circles. |
| 610-730 | solar | Mouchot's 1878 solar engine; the ring squares off into a tile, splits into a grid = a solar panel; cells mask Solaro's own install photos (Tatras rooftop, houses); cells light up orange in sequence; word **solar**. |
| 730-888 | logo | **solar** -> **Solaro** (s->S, last o arrives as the ring), type settles into the exact wordmark while the mark assembles cell by cell (ring, tile, leaf, pin); hold on lockup ~1.8 s. |

## Open questions for the panel

- Where does the "clean 3D" moment go? (extruded cells flipping like solar tiles; 3D ring/sun; 3D bulb)
- Is the open on the mark right, or should we open cold on the stone spark?
