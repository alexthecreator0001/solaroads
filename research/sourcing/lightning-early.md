# ERA 2: Lightning and early electricity (1600-1830), sourcing

- Folder: `assets/raw/lightning-early/` (40 files plus `credits.json`, which gives title, creator, date, source URL, licence, notes, pixel size and download URL for each file)
- Contact sheets: `research/sourcing/lightning-early-contact-1.jpg` and `-2.jpg`. The orange frame marks a top-8 pick.
- Every file was opened and verified with Pillow, and its size was recorded.
- Licence mix: 16 CC BY 4.0 (Wellcome, **attribution required**: "Wellcome Collection", title, CC BY 4.0) · 8 Public Domain Mark (Wellcome) · 12 public domain (NASA, LoC "no known restrictions", Figuier 1867-70 book scans) · 2 public domain by age (LoC postcards/photos from 1882 and 1914, "rights status not evaluated" by LoC) · 2 CC0 (Smithsonian, Cleveland Museum of Art). None of the files is NC, ND or SA.

Treatment key: **engraving-clean** = level the paper to #FFFFFF and threshold to line art · **cutout** = object on plain background, mask it out · **card** = keep it as a rectangular B&W card · **texture** = full-frame pattern or overlay · **trace** = paint an orange (#FF3D00 to #FF5E2B) stroke over a line.

## Notes and caveats
- Figuier scans (*Les Merveilles de la science*, vols 1 and 4, Internet Archive `lesmerveillesdel01figu_0` / `lesmerveillesdel04figu`) were cropped from full page images, with the captions removed. Many more period engravings sit on the same scans. Their djvu index pages are: 444 Guericke/Hauksbee, 456 Hausen, 463 Ramsden & Van Marum (small), 530 Dalibard 1752, 562 lightning rod struck, 610 Galvani discovery, 630 Volta builds the pile, 642 Volta before Napoleon, 674 Davy, 682 Wollaston pile, 689 crown of cups (only ~1100 px wide).
- The NASA frames come from KSC weather cameras: the long side is only 1280 px, and there is a wx logo bottom-left and a timestamp bottom-right.
- LoC postcard and photo items (1882, 1914) are public domain by age, but LoC lists their rights as "not evaluated".
- Wellcome CC BY items need on-screen or end-credit attribution.

## Gaps
- **Electric kiss (Venus electrificata):** no openly licensed print was found.
- **Faraday:** there is no Faraday disc dynamo image. The induction-ring diagram was too faint, so I dropped it.
- **Ramsden machine:** only a small Figuier figure was available, below 1200 px.
- **Wimshurst machine:** only a 1901 dental-book line drawing was found, and it is a later era anyway.
- **Davy:** I found his arc only as the vacuum-egg figure, not the classic Royal Institution arc demonstration.
- **Sources that were unavailable:**
  - NOAA Photo Library: blocked by a CAPTCHA wall.
  - Wikimedia: returned HTTP 429.
  - Smithsonian API: the DEMO_KEY was exhausted. I used the Smithsonian open-access S3 metadata instead, where most electrostatic objects have no CC0 image.
