# Era 3 - The Electric Age & the Grid (1830-1960): image sourcing

Folder: `assets/raw/electric-age/` (40 files + `credits.json` with title / creator / date / source_url / license / notes / pixel size for every file).
Contact sheets: `research/sourcing/electric-age-contact-1.jpg`, `research/sourcing/electric-age-contact-2.jpg` (same order as the table).

All files were opened with Pillow after download and their pixel sizes recorded. Formats were normalised to JPEG (TIFF / PNG / JP2 / GIF masters converted at q95; three very large LoC TIFFs were downsampled to 6000-7000 px on the long side; EXIF rotation applied).

## Sources used
Smithsonian Open Access (NMAH + Cooper Hewitt, CC0, via the public S3 open-data dump because the DEMO_KEY API was rate-limited), Wellcome Collection (PDM / CC BY), Library of Congress (Bain, Detroit Publishing, FSA/OWI, HAER - no known restrictions / US government), Google Patents drawing sheets (US patents, public domain), Old Book Illustrations (public-domain 19th-c. engravings), Internet Archive (Scientific American 1882 scan), NASA Images, The Met Open Access, Openverse (WordPress Photo Directory CC0, Flickr CC0 / PDM / CC BY).

## Top 8 picks
1. `electric-age-edison-lamp-new-type-smithsonian.jpg` - the hero bulb: 5304x7267 museum photo on white, teardrop shape = Solaro pin.
2. `electric-age-tesla-colorado-springs-sparks-wellcome.jpg` - the most iconic electricity image of the era; sparks for orange lightning paint (CC BY: credit Wellcome Collection).
3. `electric-age-patent-edison-electric-lamp-1880.jpg` - Edison's lamp patent: pure black line drawing, paint the filament orange.
4. `electric-age-pylon-silhouette-dusk-sky-wordpress.jpg` - textbook pylon silhouette, cuts out to black-on-white instantly (CC0).
5. `electric-age-earth-at-night-europe-africa-black-marble-nasa.jpg` - lit-up globe incl. Slovakia, perfect circle for ring/sun match-cut (8192 px, PD).
6. `electric-age-edison-lamps-catalog-poster-1903-loc.jpg` - ready-made grid of ~40 bulb silhouettes for a snowflake-style grid.
7. `electric-age-patent-stanley-induction-coil-1886.jpg` - three ring drawings, almost abstract = Solaro ring mark.
8. `electric-age-pearl-street-station-dynamo-room-1882-engraving-ia.jpg` - Pearl Street, the world's first central power station: the birth of the grid.

Runners-up: Tesla portrait (`tesla-portrait-wellcome`), teardrop discharge tube (`discharge-tube-teardrop-wellcome`, matches the pin shape), Tesla rotating-field patent (8-frame ring animation), Luna Park at night, the woman dressed in light bulbs (surprising human "aha").

## Gaps / what could not be filled
- **La Fee Electricite (Raoul Dufy, 1937)**: not downloaded. Dufy died in 1953, so the mural is public domain in the EU since 2024, but it is still under US copyright (1937 publication, protected until 2033), and no free high-res source turned up. Used instead: Beckwith's "Electric Light" study for the 1893 World's Fair dome (CC0) and Tenniel's "Coming Force" Punch allegory (PD).
- **Electricity Building, Chicago 1893**: LoC only has small stereo cards (about 1024 px). Covered instead by the Beckwith 1893 allegory and by Luna Park at night (1905).
- **Tesla coil photo with sparks on a clean background**: only busy modern photos were available (dropped). The 1899 Colorado Springs photo and the Tesla patent drawings cover this.
- **Early household appliances**: only the Seely electric iron patent was kept. The Technoseum CC0 appliances on Europeana (irons, AEG fans) serve 800 px at most and their IIIF images returned 404. A GE electric-fan ad (CC BY, 1200x1705) was downloaded but dropped as weak.
- **Plugs**: no clean CC0 photo of an early plug, so only the Hubbell patent drawing. Switches are covered by the bakelite grid only.
- **Vacuum tubes**: no museum-grade radio tube on white. Kept a CC0 macro photo plus 19th-century discharge / X-ray tubes on white from Wellcome.
- Wikimedia (HTTP 429) and the Smithsonian API (DEMO_KEY over its limit) were not usable. Smithsonian material came from its public S3 open-access dump instead.
