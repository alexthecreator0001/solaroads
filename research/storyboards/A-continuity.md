# A-continuity — "One continuous thought"

**Spec:** Solaro vertical spot · 1080×1920 · 60 fps · 888 frames (f0–f887 = 14.80 s) · board key `A-continuity`

> One orange shape, built only from the four Solaro cells, never leaves the screen. It is struck from stone, catches fire, falls as lightning, turns *svet* into *svetlo*, glows inside the first bulbs, circles the night Earth as the sun, squares into a solar panel and folds back into the Solaro mark. Every image sits so that its hot spot is under that shape at A = (540, 940).

---

## 1. Concept, copy, language

### Concept (3 lines)
1. **One element, one thought.** A single orange shape made only of Solaro cell geometry carries the whole history of energy: dot → ✦ → flame → bolt → letter → bulb → ring → tile → panel → mark. It never leaves the frame, and every transition is a parametric morph of it.
2. **The images orbit the element.** 84 B&W images (engravings, museum objects, NASA, Solaro's own roofs) cut every 2–6 frames. Each is placed so its hot spot (the spark, wick, kite, filament or sun disc) sits under the element at A. Every cut is a match cut on it.
3. **The logo holds the first spark.** The film opens by closing the gap in the mark so that its negative space becomes a ✦, the first spark. It ends with the same ✦ breathing once inside the finished mark. f887 = f0, so the film loops.

### Copy: every word on screen (plus the Solaro wordmark itself)
| Cut | T1 (f300–359) | T2 (f730–785) | End card |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SK (master)** | `svet` → `svetlo` | `solár` → `Solaro` | `solaro.sk` |
| EN (international alt) | `lightning` → `lighting` | `solar` → `Solaro` | `solaro.sk` |

Nothing else is written on screen. Printed titles, captions, plate numbers and dates inside source images are cropped out or retouched to paper white (§3.4).

### Language: Slovak master, English alt
- **SK is the master** because the buyer is a Slovak homeowner scrolling Instagram or Facebook. Both SK edits read at a glance and need no English:
  - *svet* + *lo* = *svetlo*: "world" becomes "light". It is a native pun, and the change is additive, so nobody misses it.
  - *solár* is the everyday Slovak word for a home solar installation, so the end gag reads "a solar system → Solaro".
- **EN alt.** *lightning → lighting* means exactly the right thing: nature's power tamed into light. But the change is one deleted *n*, and many native speakers confuse the two words, so it is the riskier read at speed. *solar → Solaro* is ideal in English.
- **Shared picture.** Picture, timing and sound are 100 % shared between the two cuts. Only the text layers in F1–F7 and J2–J4 change.
- **Sign-off needed.** *solár* is colloquial. The SK fallback is *solar*, which Slovaks understand.

## 3. Conventions (read once; the shot table uses them)

### 3.1 Coordinates & placement
- **A = (540, 940)** is the anchor and the orange element's home. **F = (540, 1034)** is the flame base: the FLAME's body centre is on A and its base is 94 px lower.
- **Placement notation.** `(fx, fy)→A s0.238 [107%W]` means the point at fraction (fx, fy) of the **raw** file (`assets/raw/…`) lands on A.
  - `s` = screen pixels per raw pixel.
  - `[%W]` = displayed image width as a percentage of 1080.
  - `TL` = the image's top-left corner on screen, given where it helps.
- **Raw fractions vs processed files.** If `tools/imgproc.py` trims or downsizes a file, the processing step must write the trim box and scale to a sidecar JSON, and anchors are converted from it. Placement is always defined on the raw file.
- **Path aliases** used in the table (full, ls-checked paths are in §7):

  | Alias | Folder |
  |---|---|
  | `sf/` | `assets/raw/spark-fire/` |
  | `le/` | `assets/raw/lightning-early/` |
  | `ea/` | `assets/raw/electric-age/` |
  | `ss/` | `assets/raw/sun-solar/` |
  | `so/` | `assets/raw/solaro/` |

- **Ambient push.** On montage holds the *image layer* zooms at a constant ratio about the anchor: ×1.008/f by default, carried across in-place swaps. The orange element and the paint-on lines stay in screen space and are never pushed.

- **Inner shadow:** only on the final mark (I9 onward).

### 3.3 Paint-on vocabulary (clean vector, no brush texture)
All ink strokes are #1C1C1C, 4 px wide, with round caps, and draw on by arc length. They always exit by **un-drawing** (in reverse), never by fading.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **P1 ORBIT** | Ink circle of radius r around A, drawn clockwise from 12 o'clock at 72°/f (5 f); un-draws in 4 f. |
| **P2 RAYS** | 8 radial ink rays from r₀ to r₁, offset 22.5°; outExpo 4 f with 0.5 f stagger; retract inExpo 3 f. |
| **P3 TRACE** | Orange `taperStroke` head (max width 20, length 150 px) running at constant speed along a hand-placed polyline (bolt channel, wire). No trail. |
| **P4 TICKS** | Four ink ticks, 24 px long, at N/E/S/W on a circle of radius r around A (a viewfinder); outCubic 3 f. |
| **P5 DIAL** | 12 ink ticks around the sun ring, radial 462→492 px, drawn clockwise at 1 tick per 0.5 f. |
| **COMET** | Orange head Ø34 with a 120 px orange taper, leaving an ink trail behind it. Used for the bolt (D11) and the drawn "l" (F3). |

### 3.4 Image treatment & text policy
| Code | Treatment |
|---|---|
| `ENG` | Engraving clean: paper → #FFFFFF, ink → #1C1C1C (`imgproc.py engrave`) |
| `CUT` | BiRefNet cutout onto white (`imgproc.py cutout`) |
| `INV` | Luminance invert + levels, for night or black-ground sources |
| `CARD` | Rectangular B&W photo, levelled to a white point (`imgproc.py photo`) |

- Every image is 100 % neutral grey: no sepia, no beige paper.
- **Text inside images.** Printed titles, captions, plate numbers, dates and signatures are cropped (*crop*) or retouched to paper white (*retouch*), as noted per shot. Engraved figure letters smaller than 20 px on screen may stay, as texture.

### 3.5 Camera, easing, blur, inversions
- **Easing names** come from `engine/core.js`: `E.snap` (.16,1,.3,1), `E.swift`, `E.whip`, `E.punch`, plus the standard in/out curves.
- **Fast scale moves** are eased in log scale: pops overshoot about +6 %, entrances are expoOut over 3 f with a 1-frame smear, push-throughs are log quadIn.
- **Motion blur** uses `film.samples(t)` with shutter 0.5. Each shot lists it as `blur n` (n sub-frames); everywhere else n = 1.
- **INVERT** is used three times, always for exactly 2 frames (B2, E1, E17): background #1C1C1C, image luminance inverted, orange untouched.

## 5. Signature moments, and why every morph is exact

1. **The first spark lives inside the logo (A1 ↔ J1).**
   - At f30 the mark's gap closes and the white space between the cells is a perfect four-point star.
   - With gap 0, the four inward corners are quarter-circles of radius R = C/2, centred on the four cell centres at (±R, ±R) from the mark centre. That boundary *is* `sparkPath(cx, cy, R)` by construction, with its points at (0, ±R) and (±R, 0).
   - So the orange ✦ that fills the space when the cells fly out (f31) is pixel-identical to the negative space: no tracing, no tolerance.
   - At f720 the finished mark closes once more and shows the same white ✦ before the lockup. The last frame is the first frame.
2. **Stone becomes fire (C9 → D1).**
   - The dot ignites *inside* an Acheulean hand-axe: one Solaro pin cell (rotated −135°, stretched 1.54) grows to 94 % of the teardrop silhouette, so the flaked stone rim stays visible as a thin outline. It then relaxes into a candle flame.
   - This is exact because DOT, FLAME-AXE and FLAME are the same cell with tweened numbers (`mixCell` plus one vertical scale). There is never an outline-to-outline match.
   - Three Roman lamps then swap under a single steady flame, registered on the nozzle. This is the reference's eye-line device, re-skinned.
3. **The ember comes back as lightning (D11 → E2).**
   - The flame collapses to the dot, the dot shoots to the top of the frame, hangs for 2 frames, and races down as a bolt (orange head, ink trail). It hits A on the beat with a 2-frame invert.
   - The cut lands on the real 1914 Spokane bolt, registered at its strike point, while an orange head re-traces its channel.
   - One object performs the whole move.
4. **The letter that turns the world into light (F, SK).**
   - The dot sits after *svet* like a full stop ("svet."), draws the **l** as a comet, arcs over and inflates into the orange **o** of *svetlo*. It then pushes through and becomes the glow inside Edison's bulb.
   - Every step is exact: circle → rectangle (the l), circle → ring (the o; the hole grows from the centre), ring → pin rotated 45° (the bulb).
   - EN: the dot eats the *n* of *lightning* and becomes the i-tittle of *lighting* (circle → pin).
5. **Night Earth → sun → cell → panel → logo (G14 → J).**
   - The dot marks Bardejov on the night Earth, orbits the globe and draws a ring. The ring's inner edge is locked to the limb, and its outer edge grows until the proportion is exactly the logo's 0.479.
   - Night cuts to day inside the ring. 22 suns accelerate down to 2 frames each.
   - Scheiner's 1612 grid of suns swaps, registered at the same disc radius, into a 1978 grid of round solar cells.
   - The ring squares into a tile (`mixCell`, radii → 0) and splits into a 6×10 panel (`gridCut` from an exact tiling, so no seam ever pops). The panel tilts in clean 3D and flips its cells to reveal Solaro's own roofs, then merges back into 2×2 and the four mark shapes (`proto_end` geometry).
   - **The Solaro mark is literally a solar module made of Solaro installs.**

**Text morphs.**
- *svet → svetlo* only adds strokes and cells.
- *solár → Solaro* is one pop (the exact wordmark *o*), one accent retracting, and one outline morph (s → S; both are single-contour S-curves). If `makeMorph` twists, fall back to a 4-frame scale swap at the same centre. Never accept a blob.
- The letters o, l, a, r are the wordmark's own paths from the first frame they appear, so the end frame *is* the logo file.

## 6. Risks & how we avoid slop

### What we deliberately do NOT do
**Transitions and effects**
- No dissolves or crossfades.
- No glows, lens flares, bloom or light leaks.
- No camera shake, random wiggle or typewriter cursor.

**Look**
- No sepia, beige paper or vignettes: every image is levelled to a #FFFFFF paper point.
- No orange tint on photos.
- No drop shadows, except the logo's own inner shadow.
- No blue "electric" plasma, plexus lines or stock energy clichés.

**Borrowed from the reference: none of these**
- Its hand-painted organic brush, dry-brush rings, serif type, asterisk, spiral line or zoetrope.

**Restraint**
- No captions, years or labels; source captions are cropped or retouched.
- Exactly one particle burst (B2, deterministic).
- Exactly one tonal chord (f780).
- Orange appears only as the element, and as the panel and mark it becomes.

### Risks and mitigations
- **Hot-spot accuracy.**
  - Fractions were read from 5 %-gridded previews and zoomed crops (±1 %).
  - Build step: a *registration sheet*, i.e. frame 0 of every shot rendered with a crosshair at A, nudged before animating (≈ 30 min). This is the single biggest quality lever.
  - Anchors are raw-file fractions, so `imgproc.py` must record trim boxes (§3.1).
- **3D stones.**
  - The GLBs are ≈ 23 MB each, Draco-compressed, with about 1.5 M vertices.
  - Decimate to about 150 k vertices, or use the PNG renders (fallback listed under B).
  - They are on screen for 26 frames only.
- **3D flip with photo back-faces (I3).** If the UV mapping takes too long, the 2D fallback (x-scale |cos θ|, face swapped at 90°) reads the same at 6 frames per cell.
- **Black Marble globe (8192²).**
  - Pre-crop it to the disc at 4096 px.
  - Bardejov ≈ (0.374, 0.276) is an estimate made from the Po valley and Adriatic positions; verify it against the Budapest and Kraków lights.
- **Monotony of an anchored montage.** It is broken up by:
  - scale jumps (Volta ×3.7, Cuthbertson ×2);
  - stepped zooms (the bulb poster, Scheiner);
  - several-at-once frames (bifaces, frogs, jars ×2, the bulb wall, the Scheiner grid, the PV cells, the four photo quadrants);
  - three 2-frame inverts, two text beats and one 3D beat.
- **Clarity at speed.** Even if no image is read, the orange icon alone tells the story: ✦ → flame → bolt → bulb → sun → panel → logo. Every era starts with a morph of it on a beat.
- **Client photos are smartphone documentation.** They appear only inside small cell windows, in B&W and levelled, where clutter reads as texture. The best photo (Tatras) is held the longest, inside the logo's ring.
- **"solár" is colloquial.** It needs client sign-off; the fallback is *solar*. "[mark] solár" is readable for about 32 frames. If the client prefers a plain logo reveal: J2 slides out the full *Solaro*, and J3–J4 merge into the hold.
- **Text inside images.**
  - Handled by the policy in §3.4.
  - Shots that need retouching: C1, E10, E11, E19, HB1 (≈ 30 min).
  - Shots that are only cropped: E5, E6, E9, G2, G3, G6, G7, G10.
- **Trim options** (if the build day runs short, ≈ 6 images): C7, E10, G8, H13, H18, H20. Each slot is absorbed by its neighbour, so the timing math is unchanged.

## 7. Build plan & asset list

### Eight mechanisms cover the whole board
1. **Anchored montage runner.** Data rows {file, hot spot, s, treatment, clip, duration} → `drawAnchored` plus a ratio push. Covers C, D, E, G and H (≈ 75 shots).
